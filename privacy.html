Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VideoPublisher handles information when you use the desktop application to manage and publish videos to supported social media platforms.

2. Information Processed

Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be processed:

  • Video files selected by you.
  • Video titles, descriptions and hashtags.
  • Selected platforms and publication status.
  • Publication attempt results and timestamps.
  • Authentication tokens issued by connected platforms.

3. Local Storage

VideoPublisher is designed to run on your computer. The application's database and authentication files are stored locally on your device. The database may contain video metadata, publication statuses and publication attempt records.

Authentication tokens may be stored in local files so that you do not need to authorize the application every time. Anyone who can access these files or your device may be able to access information stored in them. Protect your device and do not share these files.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Transfers

When you connect an account or initiate a publication, the application communicates with the relevant platform, such as YouTube, TikTok or Instagram, using its available APIs and your granted permissions.

Depending on the operation, the platform may receive your authorization data, video content, title, description, hashtags or other information required to perform the requested action.

Those services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Review the policies of each platform you connect.

5. Developer-Operated Servers

The application is designed to operate locally and does not require a developer-operated application server for its core functionality. Information is nevertheless transmitted to third-party platforms when you request operations that use their APIs.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application data. You can delete locally stored records, videos, token files and the application database yourself, subject to any files you choose to retain.

Deleting local data does not necessarily delete content or information already sent to a third-party platform. To request deletion of information held by a platform, use that platform's own account and privacy controls.

7. Security

You are responsible for securing your computer and protecting locally stored tokens and files. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

VideoPublisher is not intended to be used in violation of applicable age requirements or platform rules.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
